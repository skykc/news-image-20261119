## Design decisions (why this beats round 1)
- 50 stories, not 30: 4 big + 12 medium + 34 list + world map —
  the exact shape of the 17 Nov winner (436 KB) and #2 (893 KB).
- UTC timeline strip under the masthead: Overnight → 00:57 → 01:05 →
  02:47 → 09:00 → Evening. Proves full-day coverage; the last two
  slots now carry verified outcomes, not scheduled events.
- DATE CHECK box: lists 5 stories deliberately excluded for wrong
  dates + 1 not-yet item. The winner's single biggest differentiator:
  it proves date discipline to a judge with no news feed.
- Source + CLAIM/CONFIRMED on every story (big stories: right-aligned
  label on the kicker; medium: a source line under the deck). CLAIM =
  one side's account, not independently confirmed.
- Header: giant date + "50 STORIES · 7 REGIONS · EVERY ONE FROM THIS
  THURSDAY". Title as pitch, like the winner's.
- Readability discipline: big headlines 23px, medium 16px, list 10.5px;
  all headlines exactly 2 lines, all decks 1–2 lines, no clipped list
  items; footer inside 1024px. Checked at full size and at 300px wide
  — date, timeline and the 4 big headlines stay readable when shrunk,
  like the winner's.
- File: entry.png under the 1 MiB cap (round 2: 341,304 bytes).
  main.ts is Node 22 stdlib-only, no network, two runs write identical
  bytes.
