# NOTES — Research for "The World in One Day" (news-image-20261119)

Entry: one 1024x1024 newspaper page summing up Thursday 19 November 2026.
Target: top 50 of the field (prize zone). Mahoraga adaptation after the

## Rule used
Every headline is an event that happened, or was first reported, on 19 Nov 2026
(19 Nov 2026 WIB = 18 Nov 2026 17:00 UTC onwards is treated as "first reported"
only when the report itself is dated 19 Nov). Nothing invented, no hype as fact,
attributed forecasts stay attributed. The Gaza ground operation began on
Wednesday evening Gaza time but was first reported in the early hours of
Thursday 19 Nov (Sky News / Japan Times / AFP, 00:17–01:27 UTC) — it counts
under the "first reported" rule, and the page's rule line says so.
